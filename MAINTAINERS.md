## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers commit to:
1. Reviewing pull requests promptly and providing actionable, constructive feedback.
2. Triaging bug reports and community questions in GitHub Issues and Discussions.
3. Ensuring automated quality gates and security scans remain strictly enforced.
4. Upholding the project's [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
5. Protecting contributor privacy and coordinating vulnerability disclosures under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
